Furniture made for living

Furniro was created around a simple belief: the objects we live with should feel beautiful, useful and lasting. We work with thoughtful designers and skilled makers to create furniture that brings calm and character to everyday spaces.

From material selection to the final stitch, every detail is considered. Our collections are made to mix naturally, age gracefully and feel at home for years.
